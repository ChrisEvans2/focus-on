# Focus On peeking icon v3

Generated using built-in image_gen. Right-bottom peeking composition with open capsule eyes and flat colors. Concept only; not installed as the application icon.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand / compositing.
Generate ONE refined flat 2D Focus On app icon, modifying the composition of reference image 1.
Reference image 1 (flat icon) defines the exact dark sage pet identity, flat graphic style, ivory palette, and TWO OPEN STRAIGHT CAPSULE EYES.
Reference image 2 (screenshot of four peeking pets) is ONLY for the tilted peeking pose. IGNORE its closed eyes, winking expressions, textures, checkerboard, yellow variants and interface chrome.

New composition: a warm ivory square icon canvas. A single large dark sage rounded-square pet peeks diagonally INTO the canvas FROM THE LOWER RIGHT CORNER. Its lower and right parts extend beyond and are cropped flush by the bottom and right edges of the square canvas, as if this calm little companion is peering in to remind you to focus. The pet is rotated about 25 degrees counterclockwise, leaning toward the upper left. Upper-left area remains generous empty ivory negative space; visible pet takes up roughly the lower-right 65% of the image. Both eyes must be fully visible with comfortable clearance from the canvas edges. The two eyes are identical long ivory pill/capsule shapes, open and calm, parallel to each other, rotated together with the head. No chevrons, no smile-shaped eyes, no winking, no pupils, no mouth. Keep original capsule proportions, not dots. The silhouette should have a large rounded forehead clearly visible toward the center-left, a recognizable rotated rounded-square head, not a circle or semicircle.

Visual style: clean flat 2D graphic design. Uniform solid forest sage #496A5D main body and ivory #F7F4E9 background/eyes. A restrained hard-edged lighter sage #729180 curved accent near the cropped lower-right part of body, occupying at most 12% of the visible pet, gives a little graphic hierarchy. Crisp solid color fields, no volume, no gradient, no highlights, no drop shadow, no inner shadow, no glow, no texture, no glossy or clay material, no 3D. Use cropping and flat color overlap to create layered composition. Fully opaque square image, no transparency, no checkerboard. Exactly one standalone raster icon, no presentation board, no text, no mockups, no badges, no targets, no clocks, no frames. The lower and right canvas edges themselves crop the pet; do not draw an extra window, card, corner fold or foreground wall.
